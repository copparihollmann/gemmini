The hardware has **three independent mvin ports**: `mvin` (funct k_MVIN), `mvin2` (funct k_MVIN2), and `mvin3` (funct k_MVIN3). 4. **No change to the B scratchpad layout rule**: B base and indexing remain `b_base = BANK_NUM*BANK_ROWS - tiles_K*tiles_J*DIM` and `b_base + (k*tiles_J + j)*DIM`.
