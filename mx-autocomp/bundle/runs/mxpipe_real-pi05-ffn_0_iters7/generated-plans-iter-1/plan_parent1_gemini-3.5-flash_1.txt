### Optimization Plan

We will apply **Strategy 7: Reuse loaded B (weight) tiles across multiple I-tile iterations without re-mvin** (Loop swapping).

**Inefficiency in Current Code:**
In the original implementation, the outer loop is over `i0` (the row dimension of matrix A) and the inner loop is over `j0` (the column dimension of matrix B). While A is kept stationary in the scratchpad, B's weight scale factors and tiles are loaded repeatedly from DRAM in the inner loop. Since the systolic array is configured for `WEIGHT_STATIONARY` mode, it is more hardware-efficient to keep B (weights) stationary in the scratchpad across multiple activations (A) iterations. 

**Proposed Changes:**
1. **Swap the Loop Order**: Move the `j0` loop (column blocks of B) to the outermost level and the `i0` loop (row blocks of A) to the inner level.
2. **Hoist B Loading**: Hoist `gemmini_mx_load_scales` for B and the B matrix `gemmini_extended_mvin` calls to the outer `j0` loop. This ensures B tiles are loaded once from DRAM and reused across all `i0` iterations.
3. **Move A Loading**: Push `gemmini_mx_load_scales` for A and the A matrix `gemmini_extended_mvin` calls down to the inner `i0` loop.
4. **Consolidate Configurations**: Place `gemmini_extended3_config_ex` at the beginning of the function to eliminate redundant configuration re-issues.
