### How the Strategy Works

1. **Causal-Aware Softmax and Requantization Bypassing**: When causal attention (`#if ATT_CAUSAL`) is enabled, the upper triangle elements (where $j > i$) are masked out and mathematically guaranteed to produce a softmax probability of zero. We exploit this on the host CPU by rewriting the loops to determine the active boundaries and completely bypassing the memory loads of `S1_hw`, the call to `mx_ibf16_to_q`, the `mx_iexp` math block, and the `mx_ienc_fp8` requantization for all indices where $j > i$. This eliminates roughly half of all CPU math and memory accesses, reducing host pipeline stalls and branch mispredictions.
2. **Batch-3 Scratchpad DMA Coalescing**: The original loading of weight matrices ($K^T$ and $P_q$) batches loads into groups of 2 tiles. However, the hardware DMA controller is capable of loading up to 3 tiles at a time without exceeding the maximum column constraint (since $3 \times 16 = 48 \le 63$). By restructuring the loading to fetch tiles in groups of 3 (e.g., $3, 3, 2$), we reduce the number of issued `mvin` instructions for $K^T$ from 16 to 12, and for $P_q$ from 32 to 24.
3. **Software Cache Prefetching**: We insert `__builtin_prefetch` instructions to pull the upcoming rows of `S1_hw` and `P_q` into the CPU's cache hierarchy ahead of time while the CPU computes the softmax of the current row.
