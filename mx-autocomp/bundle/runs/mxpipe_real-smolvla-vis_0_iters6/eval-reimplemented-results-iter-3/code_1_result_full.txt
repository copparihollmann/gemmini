### Why This Is Safe

- The programming model sequence is preserved within each iteration: scales are loaded → mvin → mxquant_config → loop_ws_spad → read_smem → fence. The config_ex simply moves to a one-time setup before the first iteration.

- No data hazard is introduced: config_ex fields are stateless from the perspective of per-block data, and the fence at the end of each iteration ensures the prior block fully completes before the next block's scales are loaded.
- The architecture explicitly states: *"CONFIG_EX, CONFIG_LD, and CONFIG_ST fields persist until changed; avoid redundant re-issue when format, stride, and scale are unchanged across consecutive tiles."*
