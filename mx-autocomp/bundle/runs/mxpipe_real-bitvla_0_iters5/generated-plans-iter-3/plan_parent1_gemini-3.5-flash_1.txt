### Optimization Plan

1. **Analyze LUT Requirements for FP8 Format**:
   - The matrix multiplication uses the `fp8:e4m3` format (`act_mx_fmt = 0`, `wgt_mx_fmt = 0`, `out_mx_fmt = 3`).
   - According to the MX-Gemmini specification, the `fp8` format does not use lookup tables (LUTs), and `uselut` is set to `0` in `gemmini_extended3_config_ex`.
   - Consequently, calling `gemmini_mx_load_lut` is completely unnecessary. We will adhere to the strategy of avoiding redundant LUT reloads by ensuring that no LUT load instructions are introduced or executed.

2. **Hoist Non-Variant Configurations**:
   - We will guarantee that format configurations (`gemmini_extended3_config_ex`), scale factor loads (`gemmini_mx_load_scales`), and quantization settings (`gemmini_mxquant_config_mvout`) are kept outside of any data loading loops. This prevents the hardware from executing redundant configuration instructions, saving valuable cycles.

3. **Maintain Memory Layout and Alignment**:
   - Keep the scratchpad allocation intact (`a_base` and `b_base` at the top of the scratchpad) to prevent bank overlapping.
   - Use the explicit `gemmini_extended_mvin` instructions to load A and B tiles.
   - Execute the hardware-fused `gemmini_loop_ws_spad` once on the loaded scratchpad.
   - Drain the results from `mx_smem` to DRAM and synchronize with `gemmini_fence()`.
