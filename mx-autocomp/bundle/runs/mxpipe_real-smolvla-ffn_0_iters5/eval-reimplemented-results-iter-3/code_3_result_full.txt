2. **Inefficient Scratchpad Writing:** Because B tiles are loaded individually, the compiler cannot coalesce the `mvin` instructions. This results in a massive number of individual DMA commands being issued, introducing significant control-flow overhead.

3. **Contiguous Scratchpad Layout:** In accordance with **Rule 12**, we lay out B tiles in the scratchpad at `b_base + (j * tiles_K + k) * DIM`. Under this layout, tiles along the $K$ dimension are stored contiguously in the scratchpad.
