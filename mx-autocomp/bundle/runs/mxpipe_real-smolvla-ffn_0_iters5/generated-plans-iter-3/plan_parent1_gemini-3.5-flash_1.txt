### Optimization Plan

1. **Analyze the Inefficiencies**:

   - On this accelerator, host CPU memory-copy operations (`memcpy`) and repeated small DMA configurations completely dominate runtime.

2. **Apply Strategy 5 (Batching Scale Factor Loads)**:

   - We will entirely hoist and consolidate the scale factor loads outside of all loops.
   - We will load the entire scale matrices (`A_scales_row` and `B_scales_col`) once at the beginning of the program using `gemmini_mx_load_scales` with `sizeof(A_scales_row)` and `sizeof(B_scales_col)`.
