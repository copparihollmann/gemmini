An elegant and highly effective optimization plan to address the performance bottleneck is **Strategy 1: Loop Reordering and Restructuring**.

---

### Inefficiency Analysis

In the provided code, the outer loop iterates over activation row blocks (`i0`), and the inner loop iterates over weight column blocks (`j0`). 
* For every single `i0` block, the inner loop runs `BLOCKS_N` times.
* Consequently, for every iteration of `j0`, weight scale factors are staged via `memcpy` and loaded via `gemmini_mx_load_scales`, and weight matrix (B) tiles are loaded via `gemmini_extended_mvin`.
* Because B is loaded in the innermost loop, it is redundantly fetched from DRAM `BLOCKS_M` times. Under a **Weight-Stationary (`WEIGHT_STATIONARY`)** dataflow, this is highly inefficient. Weights should remain stationary in the scratchpad to maximize reuse.

---

### Optimization Plan: Loop Reordering and Restructuring

By swapping the loop order—making the weight column block loop (`j0`) the outer loop and the activation row block loop (`i0`) the inner loop—we can cache and reuse weight tiles in the scratchpad across all activation blocks.

#### Step-by-Step Implementation Plan:

1. **Invert the Loop Nest**:
   * Change the outer loop to iterate over `j0` from `0` to `BLOCKS_N`.
   * Change the inner loop to iterate over `i0` from `0` to `BLOCKS_M`.

2. **Hoist Weight-Side Configurations and Loads to the Outer Loop**:
   * Move the weight scale staging (`B_scales_col` to `B_s` `memcpy`) and `gemmini_mx_load_scales(..., 1)` to the outer `j0` loop. This loads weight scales exactly once per column block.
   * Move the weight tile MVIN block (stride config and `gemmini_extended_mvin` loop for B) into the outer `j0` loop, placing the loaded weight tiles at `b_base` in the scratchpad.

3. **Sink Activation-Side Configurations and Loads to the Inner Loop**:
   * Move the activation scale staging (`A_scales_row` to `A_s` `memcpy`) and `gemmini_mx_load_scales(..., 0)` into the inner `i0` loop.
   * Keep the activation tile MVIN block (stride config and `gemmini_extended_mvin` loop for A) inside the inner `i0` loop, landing them at `a_base`.

4. **Retain Inner-Loop Execution Flow**:
   * Keep the computation configuration (`gemmini_config_st`, `gemmini_mxquant_config_mvout`), the fused hardware loop compute (`gemmini_loop_ws_spad`), and the SMEM read-out flow (`gemmini_mx_read_smem` + `gemmini_fence()`) in the innermost body (now indexed by `i0` and `j0`).

#### Impact:
This restructuring completely eliminates the redundant DRAM-to-Scratchpad transfers of the weight matrix and weight scales. Instead of loading weights `BLOCKS_M * BLOCKS_N` times, weights are loaded only `BLOCKS_N` times. This drastically cuts down on DMA latency and scale-loading overhead, unlocking the full potential of the weight-stationary systolic array.
