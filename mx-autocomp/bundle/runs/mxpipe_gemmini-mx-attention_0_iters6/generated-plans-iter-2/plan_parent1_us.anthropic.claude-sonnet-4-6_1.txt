int e = mx_ilog2((uint64_t)gmax) - ls - MX_P_TARGET_LOG2;

**Apply Strategy 2: Fix the P scale target to prevent e4 accumulator overflow in P@V.**

The fix is to ensure `MX_P_TARGET_LOG2` equals 4 in the scale computation:

```c
int e = mx_ilog2((uint64_t)gmax) - ls - MX_P_TARGET_LOG2;
```

If `MX_P_TARGET_LOG2` is currently defined as something larger (e.g., 8 to target fp8 max ~448), change it to 4. This ensures the group-max of P maps to ~2^4=16, keeping FP8 encoded values moderate enough that accumulating 128 of them (tiles_K=8 for ATT_S=128) in the e4 block accumulator doesn't overflow.

- Locate or define `MX_P_TARGET_LOG2 = 4` (if it's currently a different value or implicitly large)
