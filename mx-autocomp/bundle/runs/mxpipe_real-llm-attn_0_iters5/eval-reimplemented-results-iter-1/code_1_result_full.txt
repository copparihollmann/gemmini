1. **Scale Loading Hoisting**: The host-side `memcpy` for `A_scales_row` and the `gemmini_mx_load_scales(..., 0)` instruction are executed once per $i_0$ block, saving $BLOCKS\_N - 1$ redundant scale transfers per $i_0$ iteration.
