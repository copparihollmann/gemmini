## Analysis

Looking at the code, the softmax section processes rows in batches of 4, with three passes over the data:
1. Unpack + find max per row
2. Compute iexp + sum, store `p[r][j]`
3. For each group g, scan `p[r][j]` again to find `gmax`, then encode fp8

The strategy **#11** directly addresses the inefficiency: the scale-computation inner loop (step 3) does a separate scan over `p[r][j]` for each of the `GROUPS_S` groups to find `gmax`, but we already have all the `p[r][j]` values after step 2. We can eliminate this redundant third pass by computing per-group maximums **during** the iexp+sum loop (step 2) — incrementally tracking the group max alongside accumulating the sum.

## Plan: Fuse Group Max Computation into the iexp+sum Loop

**Current inefficiency:** After computing `p[r][j]` in the iexp+sum loop, the code loops over all groups `g` (0..GROUPS_S-1), and for each group does an inner loop `for (int j = jstart; j < jend; j++)` to find `gmax` from `p[r][j]`. This is an O(GROUPS_S × 32 × 4) = O(ATT_S × 4) scan that repeats what was already traversed in the iexp loop.

**Fix:** Introduce a `gmax[4][GROUPS_S]` array initialized to 0. During the existing iexp+sum loop over `j`, after computing `pv0..pv3` and storing into `p[r][j]`, also update `gmax[r][j/32]` if `pv_r > gmax[r][j/32]`. Then in the group-processing loop, remove the inner `gmax` scan and use `gmax[r][g]` directly.

**Concrete changes:**
1. Before the iexp+sum loop, declare `int64_t gmax[4][GROUPS_S]` initialized to 0.
2. Inside the `j` loop body, after `p[r][j] = pvr`, add: `if (pvr > gmax[r][j/32]) gmax[r][j/32] = pvr;` for each of the 4 rows. Since `j` increments by 1 and groups are contiguous 32-element blocks, `j/32` gives the group index with no extra branching needed. Since `j` goes 0..ATT_S-1 and `GROUPS_S = ATT_S/32`, this is correct.
3. In the group loop, remove `int64_t gmax = 0;` and the `for (int j = jstart; j < jend; j++) if (p[r][j] > gmax) gmax = p[r][j];` scan entirely.
4. Replace the local `gmax` variable in the group loop with `gmax[r][g]`.

This eliminates the O(GROUPS_S × 32) = O(ATT_S) redundant scan per batch of 4 rows, replacing it with a single fused pass. The semantic result is identical because `gmax[r][g]` will contain the maximum of `p[r][jstart..jend-1]` by the time it is used, exactly as before.

Since `j/32` is an integer division by a constant power of 2, the compiler will optimize it to a shift, adding negligible overhead to the iexp loop while eliminating an entire O(ATT_S) pass per row batch.
