### Strategy: Hoist Invariant `A` Matrix DMA, Scales, and Configurations
In the original code, the `A` matrix block and its corresponding per-group scale factors depend exclusively on the outer `i0` loop. However, they are redundantly staged and DMA-loaded (`mvin`) inside the inner `j0` loop. This means the accelerator redundantly fetches the exact same `A` matrix tiles from DRAM `BLOCKS_N` times for every outer loop iteration. 

Because the Gemmini scratchpad is 256KB, it safely holds both the `A` block (placed at `a_base = 0`) and the `B` block (placed near the top at `b_base`) without them overlapping.
