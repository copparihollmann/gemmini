## Plan: Apply Strategy 10

**What to change:** In the softmax CPU loop, replace the conditional nibble-packing:
```c
if ((i & 1) == 0)
  P_hw[i >> 1][j] = (P_hw[i >> 1][j] & 0xF0) | idx;
else
  P_hw[i >> 1][j] = (P_hw[i >> 1][j] & 0x0F) | (idx << 4);
```

With two separate passes:
1. **Pre-zero `P_hw`** before the softmax loop using `memset(P_hw, 0, sizeof(P_hw))`.
2. **Even rows (`i` even):** directly OR the low nibble: `P_hw[i >> 1][j] |= idx;` (no mask needed since byte is 0 or only high nibble is set).
3. **Odd rows (`i` odd):** directly OR the high nibble: `P_hw[i >> 1][j] |= (idx << 4);`

Since `P_hw` is pre-zeroed, we can simplify to `|=` without masking. The branch is eliminated entirely. We can also **separate the single loop into two loops** — one for even `i` (0, 2, 4, ...) and one for odd `i` (1, 3, 5, ...) — making each loop unconditional and enabling better compiler optimization (vectorization, simplified addressing).

**Specifically:** Split the `for (int i = 0; i < ATT_S; i++)` softmax loop's packing section into two separate outer loops over even and odd rows, eliminating the `(i & 1)` branch on every `j` iteration. This reduces branch mispredictions and read-modify-write conflicts across `ATT_S * GKP * 32 = 128 * 4 * 32 = 16384` iterations of the inner packing body.
